نبذة عن الطبيب

Dr. Viney Jetley is a highly experienced cardiologist with over 37 years of experience. He has successfully performed over 10,000 interventional procedures across various hospitals throughout India, including complex coronary angioplasties and more than 1,000 radiofrequency ablations. His areas of expertise include invasive cardiac procedures such as angiography, stenting, rotablation, and device implantations, including pacemakers, COMBO devices, and electrophysiology treatments. Dr. Jetley also has extensive experience in pediatric cardiac catheterizations and interventions, such as balloon atrial septostomy, balloon dilatation of stenotic valves, coarctation stenting, and device closures for atrial septal defect (ASD). He was awarded the prestigious Vishist Seva Medal by the President of India for his outstanding work in interventional cardiology and electrophysiology. Dr. Viney Jetley communicates with patients and their families in a way that brings comfort and ease. His approachable manner and genuine care help patients, especially children, feel supported throughout their treatment.
